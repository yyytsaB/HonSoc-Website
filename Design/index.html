"In Scientia Veritas, In Virtute Prudentia"

In Knowledge, Truth; In Virtue, Wisdom. Established in 2020 at the College of Arts and Sciences, Batangas State University Pablo Borbon Main I, the CAS Honor Society is the recognized autonomous governing body dedicated to recognizing, evaluating, and uplifting scholastic excellence.
