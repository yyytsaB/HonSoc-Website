Background of the Organization

The official governing body of academics and scholars under the College of Arts and Sciences at Batangas State University Pablo Borbon Main I Campus. Consigned to uphold intellectual rigor, scholarly integrity, and institutional honor across all degree disciplines.

Established as an autonomous scholastic organization, the society unites honor roll recipients, university scholars, and academic achievers into a collegial community committed to high scholarship, peer mentorship, and civic transformation across Batangas province.

01.

Vision of the Society

A functioning and governing body of students from the College of Arts and Sciences department consigned to uphold the intellectual excellence of the students, as well as to improve and nurture pride in and for the academic community.

02.

Mission of the Society

The organization intends to be the governing force for the academic community of the College of Arts and Sciences, by uplifting the integrity of the students in all their future scholarly endeavors inside and outside of the University. It promotes the dissemination of acquired knowledge and encourages its members to continue to hone their intellect and abilities in order to be vital members of a working and productive community.

Legal & Institutional Basis

Constitutional Charter & By-Laws

Ratified by the General Assembly under the supervision of the Office of Student Organizations (OSO).

Article II: Scholastic Mandate

Ratified

Section 1 defines the society as the sole recognized scholastic umbrella organization of the College of Arts and Sciences, vested with the authority to curate, evaluate, and endorse candidate rosters for Dean’s List honors to the University Registrar.

Article IV: General Assembly

Ratified

Section 3 mandates bi-annual plenary sessions of all enrolled honor roll recipients to deliberate upon society fiscal budgets, constitutional amendments, and election protocols for the incoming Student Executive Secretariat.

Article VII: Student Welfare

Ratified

Section 2 establishes the Scholar Welfare Fund, allocating a minimum of 25% of all society revenue directly to indigent student grants, academic review allowances, and community educational drives across Batangas province.
