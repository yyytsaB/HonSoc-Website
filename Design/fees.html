Audit Resolution & Remittance Protocol

Full disclosure of executive resolutions, payment verification steps, and authorized channels.

Executive Audit Docket
RES. NO. 2024-03

"Be it enacted by the Student Executive Secretariat of the College of Arts and Sciences Honor Society, with concurrence from the Office of Student Organizations..."

SECTION 1. ZERO PERSONAL PECUNIARY BENEFIT. In adherence to University Student Handbook Chapter IV, no officer, adviser, or member shall receive any stipend, honorarium, or personal benefit from the membership dues collection. 100% of realized collections are restricted to direct academic operations.

SECTION 2. SEMESTRAL AUDIT DISCLOSURE. A certified financial ledger detailing all expenditures, bank balances, and disbursements shall be published on this portal within fifteen (15) calendar days following the conclusion of each academic semester.

SECTION 3. INDIGENT SCHOLAR EXEMPTION. Any student classified as an active 4Ps beneficiary, university student assistant, or barangay indigent scholar is automatically granted a 100% dues waiver upon submission of a Certificate of Eligibility.

Official Remittance Protocol

How to Verify Your Membership

Follow this 3-step verification process to receive your official electronic receipt and active status certification.

1

Remit via Authorized Channel

Pay ₱75 (Semestral) or ₱130 (Annual) over-the-counter at CAS Secretariat Desk (Room 302, CAS Building) or via authorized university remittance channels with your Student SR-Code as the payment note.

2

Submit Verification Slip

Upload a clear screenshot of the transaction slip or physical receipt through the official portal verification gateway or present it directly to the Secretariat Desk within 48 hours of payment.

3

Claim Official Stamped E-Receipt

The Committee on Records audits your reference number within 3 business days, updating your status in the active Honor Scholar Directory and releasing your e-receipt.
